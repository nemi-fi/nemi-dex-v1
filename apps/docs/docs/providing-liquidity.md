# Providing Liquidity

Earn fees from every swap by adding tokens to pools at [nemi.fi/pool](https://nemi.fi/pool).

{/* :::info Returns
APR = (Daily Fees ÷ TVL) × 365
::: */}

## Add Liquidity

1. Go to [nemi.fi/pool](https://nemi.fi/pool)
2. Find a pool and click **Add Liquidity**
3. Enter Token A amount
4. Token B auto-calculates (or enter manually for new pools)
5. Click **Add Liquidity**
6. Confirm in wallet → Wait for ZK proof (30-60s)

You receive **LP tokens** representing your pool share.

## Remove Liquidity

1. Go to [nemi.fi/pool](https://nemi.fi/pool)
2. Click **My Pools** tab
3. Click **Remove** on your position
4. Enter LP amount or click **Max**
5. Click **Remove Liquidity**
6. Confirm → Receive both tokens + earned fees

## Impermanent Loss

When prices change, you may have less than just holding.

| Price Change | IL    |
| ------------ | ----- |
| 50%          | ~2%   |
| 100% (2x)    | ~5.7% |

:::tip Minimize IL

- Use **stable pairs** (USDC/USDT)
- Hold **long-term** (fees offset IL over time)
- Choose **high-volume** pools
  :::

## FAQ

**How long to LP?**
30+ days minimum. 90+ days for fees to offset IL.

**Can I lose money?**
Yes — IL, token depreciation, smart contract risk.

**Single-sided liquidity?**
No, must provide both tokens.
